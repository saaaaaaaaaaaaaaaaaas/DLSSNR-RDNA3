NR Control: Ditto SR trial update (Linux/Proton, x86_64, RDNA3)

Requires the existing RDNA3 0.0.8.3 port package and OptiScaler route.
Exit the game. Back up OptiScaler/plugins/dlssnr-amd.asi and the existing
dlssnr-amd/shaders/renodx folder outside the game folder.
Copy this archive's OptiScaler and dlssnr-amd folders into the game folder,
merging them with the existing folders. Replace the ASI and all five Ditto
shaders together; the old prepare shader is incompatible with this update.

In NR Control (End), choose a RenoDX layer and set:
  Placement: Before the upscaler
  Resolution scale: for example 0.33
  Upsampling: Ditto SR
Keep your preferred upscaler, such as FSR 4, selected in OptiScaler.

At 1280x720 SR input, 0.33 runs NR at 422x238. Ditto SR reconstructs neural
to up_neural at 1280x720. NR's normal resolve uses up_proxy and up_neural;
then OptiScaler's SR can upscale the composed result to 1920x1080.
At scale 1, Ditto SR has no enlargement to do and the normal NR path runs.
Follow render resolution overrides Resolution scale; disable it for this example.
Ditto SR needs the game's readable depth, motion vectors and jitter.
Unsupported placement or missing inputs are reported in NR Control.

Build and software Vulkan checks passed. AMD GPU image quality, timing,
frame generation and the in-game FSR 4 chain still require a target trial.
No model, NVIDIA DLL, OptiScaler or frame-generation provider is included.

Rollback: exit the game and restore the backed-up ASI and shader folder.
