# Preserved RDNA4 DittoSR 0.4.0 shaders

These files are byte-identical to the previously integrated DittoSR v0.4.0
(commit d37519ce85a84a4cc3083cad17a83335bee0f6d4), plus its integration prepare
shader, as recorded at fork b0ec753. They are selected only by the RDNA4 build.
DittoSR's MIT and GSR2 BSD notices are in `../dittosr/` and shipped by the builder.
